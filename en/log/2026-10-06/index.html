Night of 6–7 October 2026

4 objects · 5 h 31 min of light · 20:18–06:32 · Moon 16%

The telescope worked from 20:18 to 06:32 and shot Pacman Nebula, Tadpoles Nebula, Rosette Nebula and Moon, gathering 5 h 31 min of deep-sky light.

The Moon was a waning crescent at 16% illumination.

Forecast for the night: partly cloudy, average cloud cover 57%, down to 11.9 °C, wind up to 1.4 m/s. This is the forecast saved before the night, not a measurement.
