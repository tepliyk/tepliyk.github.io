  2. 2The telescope shoots at nightThe DWARF Mini follows its plan while I sleep. The best hours are after midnight.

About me and this balcony

I'm Kostia. By day I write code, by night I collect the light of nebulae and galaxies. I've had the telescope since August 2026: a small DWARF Mini smart telescope that points itself, tracks the object and stacks the frames on its own.

My conditions are, frankly, so-so. The balcony roof hides half the sky, so I only get its eastern part. Until midnight everything is washed out by street lights, and even after that the city sky stays bright. So every image here is hours of light gathered over several nights, followed by a long processing session.

How I shoot, what worked and what didn't: all of that is in my Telegram channel «Nebo Blyzhche» («The Sky, Closer»). It's in Ukrainian, but questions in English are welcome too.
